## Scientific scope

The paper independently reproduces the two-agent cubic relation reported as Empirical Law 4.3 in Berg Thomsen, Taylor, and Dieuleveut and analyzes its sensitivity to heterogeneous local regularity under communication compression. The manuscript calls the reproduced relation the **Heterogeneity-Aware Convergence Law (HAC Law)**.

The main numerical and symbolic evidence recorded in the submitted manuscript includes:

- the complete **10,500-configuration** source-grid reproduction;
- a **216,027-configuration** equal-smoothness sensitivity audit;
- **273,885** requested full-regularity cells, including **258,400** admissible cells;
- **20,000** additional off-grid robustness pairs;
- the mismatch coordinate \(\Psi=K_1-K_2\), interpreted as the weighted variance of local regularity ratios;
- symbolic selected-root sensitivity for the stated positive-compression domain.

The manuscript explicitly limits its claims to consequences of the reproduced empirical relation and does not present a new general EF21 convergence theorem.
