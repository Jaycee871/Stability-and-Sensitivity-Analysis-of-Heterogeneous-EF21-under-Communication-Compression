# Novelty Statement

The manuscript extends, rather than repeats, the independent reproduction of Empirical Law 4.3 from *A Tight Theory of Error Feedback Algorithms in Distributed Optimization*. Its novelty is the controlled characterization built around that inherited cubic law.

The extension has four linked components. First, a fixed-average two-agent parameterization varies local-curvature heterogeneity while holding average strong convexity constant, separating heterogeneity from baseline conditioning. Second, a 216,027-configuration study maps the joint effects of heterogeneity, compression error, and average conditioning and expresses the resulting slowdown through normalized contraction-margin loss. Third, the analysis converts this landscape into retention-based operating boundaries and verifies their numerical robustness through grid refinement and 20,000 deterministic off-grid paired checks per conditioning stratum. Fourth, an independent Wolfram symbolic audit establishes a positive cubic discriminant—and hence three distinct real roots—throughout each of the three tested fixed-conditioning open domains.

Accordingly, the contribution is a reproducible numerical and mathematical sensitivity characterization with parameter-selection guidance and root-structure analysis. It does not claim a proof of the inherited empirical law or a general EF21 convergence theorem.
