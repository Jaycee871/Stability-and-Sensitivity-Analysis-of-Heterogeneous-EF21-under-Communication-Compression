# Cover letter draft

Dear Editors of *Mathematics*,

Please consider our manuscript, **“Stability and Sensitivity Analysis of Heterogeneous EF21 under Communication Compression,”** for publication as an Article in the Special Issue **“Artificial Intelligence and Algorithms.”**

The manuscript builds on the recent two-agent heterogeneous error-feedback convergence law reported in *A Tight Theory of Error Feedback Algorithms in Distributed Optimization*. Rather than proposing a new algorithm or claiming a general convergence theorem, we isolate a controlled heterogeneous regime in which average conditioning is held fixed and systematically characterize how worker heterogeneity and communication compression jointly affect the inherited cubic contraction factor.

The study contributes (i) a fixed-average parameterization that separates heterogeneity from baseline conditioning, (ii) a 216,027-cell stability and sensitivity analysis across three conditioning strata, (iii) contraction-margin retention boundaries with off-grid and grid-refinement validation, and (iv) an independent symbolic audit showing a strictly positive cubic discriminant on each of the three fixed-conditioning open domains studied. The complete computational workflow is reproducible from the accompanying repository.

We believe the manuscript is well aligned with the Special Issue because it combines mathematical analysis, algorithmic optimization, computational verification, and communication-efficient distributed learning.

We confirm that neither this manuscript nor any part of its substantive content is currently under consideration for publication in another journal, and the manuscript has not been published previously in journal form. All authors will approve the final submitted version and accept responsibility for the integrity of the work.
