# Scoped Contribution and Novelty-Guardrail Statement

The manuscript extends, rather than repeats, an independent reproduction of Empirical Law 4.3 from *A Tight Theory of Error Feedback Algorithms in Distributed Optimization*. The cubic, `K1`, `K2`, and the empirical optimal step-size expression are inherited from that source and are not presented as new results.

The current contribution has two linked layers. The equal-smoothness baseline fixes average conditioning, varies strong-convexity heterogeneity, and maps the interaction with communication compression over 216,027 controlled configurations. It expresses slowdown through contraction-margin loss, derives retention-based operating boundaries, checks boundary refinement, and performs deterministic off-grid robustness tests.

The full-regularity layer releases the equal-smoothness restriction while fixing the arithmetic means of both local smoothness and local strong convexity. In that controlled parameterization, the inherited empirical step size and `K2` are invariant. The identity `K1-K2 = Var_w(q_i)` is treated explicitly as an inherited algebraic reinterpretation, not as a novelty claim. The controlled factorization of `K1-K2` in `(kappa_bar,tau_L,tau_mu)` exposes `(tau_L-tau_mu)^2` as the mismatch factor, so proportional regularity heterogeneity leaves the inherited cubic unchanged. A separate symbolic analysis of the inherited cubic establishes the stated generic root structure and positive largest-root sensitivity with respect to `K1`; conditional on Empirical Law 4.3, admissible regularity mismatch therefore worsens the predicted contraction factor.

The literature audit supports scoped wording only. The named source-paper reference chain was closed by primary-source inspection, but `universal_novelty_certified=false` is retained in the machine-readable audit. Accordingly, the manuscript may state what it derives, shows, and establishes within the inherited `n=2` cubic, but it does not use absolute precedence claims such as “first,” “for the first time,” or “no prior work.”

The intended contribution is therefore a reproducible computational, algebraic, symbolic, and literature-audited characterization of an inherited empirical object. It does not claim a proof of Empirical Law 4.3 itself, a general EF21 convergence theorem, or novelty of generic polynomial-root, discriminant, Jury/Routh, or implicit-differentiation techniques.
